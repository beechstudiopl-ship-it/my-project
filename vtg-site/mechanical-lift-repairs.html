Signs of a mechanical fault

Call us if your lift shows any of these. Mechanical wear gets worse with every trip.

  • Grinding, knocking or humming from the machine room or shaft
  • Jerky starts, rough stops or vibration in the car
  • The car stops above or below the floor level
  • Oil on the machine or the floor around the gearbox
  • Visible wear, rust or broken wires on the ropes
  • The lift slows down, overheats or trips out during busy periods
