Why our lift engineers fix it first time

A lift fails at one component. We send the people who work on that component every day, and they find the cause instead of resetting the symptom.

  • The right engineer for the fault

    Door faults go to the door team, controller faults to electrical. Mixed faults get both.

  • Cause, not reset

    A lift that runs again after a reset is still faulty. We trace the cause and tell you what failed and why.

  • Written quote, then work

    You get the diagnosis and a price before we start. Nothing beyond the agreed scope without your approval.

Questions about our lift engineers

What do your lift engineers repair?

Every part of the lift, through four specialist companies: mechanical repairs (traction machines, brakes, gearboxes, ropes), lift door systems (car and landing doors, operators, locks), electrical fault finding (controllers, drives, safety circuits, wiring) and modernisation (control upgrades, obsolete parts, car refurbishment).

Who do you work with?

Lift service firms, facilities managers, managing agents and building owners across London.

Do you work for other lift companies?

Yes. Lift service firms are one of the groups we work for, when a fault needs a door, drive or controller specialist under their contract.

How do you decide which engineer comes?

Each company focuses on one part of the lift, and work is shared across the team by skills and availability. The fault goes to the team that works on that system every day.

Is a LOLER thorough examination the same as maintenance?

No. Passenger lifts need a thorough examination by a competent person at least every six months, in addition to regular maintenance. We carry out the repairs an examination report calls for.
