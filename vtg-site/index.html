Why a specialist team fixes it first time

A lift fails at one component. We send the people who work on that component every day, and they find the cause instead of resetting the symptom.

  • The right engineer for the fault

    Door faults go to the door team, controller faults to electrical. Mixed faults get both.

  • Cause, not reset

    A lift that runs again after a reset is still faulty. We trace the cause and tell you what failed and why.

  • Written quote, then work

    You get the diagnosis and a price before we start. Nothing beyond the agreed scope without your approval.

“Lifts don’t fail in general. They fail at one component. That’s why we built four specialist companies instead of one general team. When you call us, the engineer who comes works on that part every day, tells you plainly what failed, and gives you a price before anything is touched.”

[Owner name] Co-founder, The Vertical Transportation Group

From fault report to lift back in service

The same four steps on every job, whether it is one stuck door or a controller replacement.

  1. 01

    Report the fault

    Call or send the form: building, lift, symptoms. We confirm who is coming and when.

  2. 02

    Diagnose on site

    A specialist engineer finds the failed component and makes the lift safe.

  3. 03

    Quote and approve

    You receive the findings and a written price. Work starts when you approve it.

  4. 04

    Repair, test, report

    We repair, test the lift and send a job report for your records.
