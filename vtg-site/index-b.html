Why a specialist team fixes it first time

A lift fails at one component. We send the people who work on that component every day, and they find the cause instead of resetting the symptom.

  • The right engineer for the fault

    Door faults go to the door team, controller faults to electrical. Mixed faults get both.

  • Cause, not reset

    A lift that runs again after a reset is still faulty. We trace the cause and tell you what failed and why.

  • Written quote, then work

    You get the diagnosis and a price before we start. Nothing beyond the agreed scope without your approval.

  • Repair today, plan for tomorrow

    Where parts are obsolete we say so, and price a partial modernisation alongside the repair.

From fault report to lift back in service

The same four steps on every job, whether it is one stuck door or a controller replacement.

  1. 01

    Report the fault

    Call or send the form: building, lift, symptoms. We confirm who is coming and when.

  2. 02

    Diagnose on site

    A specialist engineer finds the failed component and makes the lift safe.

  3. 03

    Quote and approve

    You receive the findings and a written price. Work starts when you approve it.

  4. 04

    Repair, test, report

    We repair, test the lift and send a job report for your records.

Questions we are asked

Do you repair lifts maintained by another company?

Yes. We repair lifts of all makes, whoever maintains them, and we also work directly for lift service companies as specialist support.

Do I need a maintenance contract to call you out?

No. Report the fault and we will attend, diagnose and quote. [Confirm call-out terms and charges.]

How quickly can an engineer attend?

[Confirmed response time and hours, e.g. same-day attendance across Greater London.] Trapped passengers are always the first priority: call +44 7851 751 556.

Should we repair the lift or modernise it?

It depends on the condition of the equipment and whether parts are still available. After the diagnosis we can price the repair and a partial modernisation side by side, so you can decide on cost and lifetime.

Is a LOLER thorough examination the same as maintenance?

No. Passenger lifts need a thorough examination by a competent person at least every six months, in addition to regular maintenance. We carry out the repairs an examination report calls for and document them.
